Calculate a weighted rating  : យើងធ្វើការគណនាដោយ គិតពីចំនួន អ្នក Voting and Rating 

Example: 3 peopel are voting 5 rating then it will calculate the Weighted rating 

WR = (v ÷ (v+m)) × R + (m ÷ (v+m)) × C 

v - number of votes for a movie ចំនួនអ្នកបោះឆ្នោត

m - minimum number of votes required កំរិតចំនួនការបោះឆ្នោត 

R - average rating of the movie​ កំរិតមធ្យោមនៃការបោះឆ្នោត 5.2 or 7.0

C - average rating across all movies
